## Part 2: Ghana ECG Electricity Theft Problem

### The Global Problem
- **Global loss**: $96 billion/year to electricity theft
- **Ghana**: Part of the 20-50% loss rates seen in developing countries
- **Methods in Ghana**: 
  - Direct hooking from line
  - Bypassing the energy meter
  - Injecting foreign element in meter (remote slowdown)
  - Physical obstruction of meters
  - ESD attack on electronic meters

### Why This Matters
- Illegal connections cause safety hazards (fires, electrocution)
- Lost revenue = higher tariffs for paying customers
- In Ghana, this is called "kicking" or "meter hacking"

## Part 3: How Other Countries Solve It

### Country Solutions

| Country/Region | Solution | Key Approach | Cost |
|----------------|----------|--------------|------|
| **Brazil** | Smart Grid + Smart Meters | Moved meters outside (pole-top), self-diagnostic real-time monitoring | High |
| **India** | AI/ML Detection | Machine learning analyzes consumption patterns | Medium |
| **Turkey** | Regionalized Approach | Targeted deployment in high-theft provinces (70%+) | Medium |
| **USA/Europe** | Smart Meters | Auto-alert on tampering, remote disconnect | High |
| **Pakistan** | Combination | Meter upgrades + enforcement | Medium |

### Key Insights from Brazil
- Moved meters OUTSIDE homes - first step toward fraud prevention
- Smart meters have self-diagnostic tools
- Real-time monitoring can pinpoint theft location
- ROI within 2-3 years by eliminating illegal connections
- "Revenue assurance" is top driver for utilities

### Methods of Detection
1. **AI/Machine Learning** - Detect unusual consumption patterns
2. **Smart Meter Tamper Alerts** - Auto-notification
3. **Physical Inspection** - Traditional but effective
4. **Network Analysis** - Compare supply vs consumption
5. **Neighborhood Comparison** - Flag anomalies vs similar households
